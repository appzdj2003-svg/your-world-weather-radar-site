⚡ Generators & generator safety

Keep the fridge, lights and phones going through a long outage.

Generator safety: Run a generator outdoors only, far from windows, doors and vents. Never run one indoors, in a garage, basement or shed, even with the door open. Exhaust has carbon monoxide, which you can't see or smell. Never plug a generator into a wall outlet; use extension cords or a transfer switch installed by a licensed electrician.
