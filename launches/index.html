🔭 Watching a launch

⏱️

Times slip. Check before you go

Launch times move often, and scrubs on the day are common. Check the launch provider's website or social accounts for the latest time before you head out.

🌅

Twilight launches put on the best show

Shortly after sunset or before sunrise, sunlight can still hit the exhaust plume high up while the sky is dark where you are, which can make it glow. It depends on the time and the sky.

🔭

Distance and the sound delay

You can see a launch from far away when the sky is clear, but the rocket looks small. Binoculars help. If you're close enough to hear it, the rumble arrives seconds after liftoff.

🏖️

Florida's Space Coast

Around Cape Canaveral and Kennedy, people watch from public parks, beaches and riverfronts in places like Titusville, Cocoa Beach and Port Canaveral. Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex sells viewing tickets for some launches.

🌉

Vandenberg and Starbase

Vandenberg launches are often visible from many parts of Southern California when the sky is clear. Near Starbase, the road and beach close for launches; follow the local closure notices.

🚧

Respect closures

Stay out of closed roads, beaches and restricted areas, and don't fly drones near launch sites. If you can't be there, the provider's webcast is the best seat.
