Most of the app is free. Premium adds longer briefings, hurricane and hazard trackers, and Close-One.

Live radar Free

Rain in classic colors with snow kept light blue. Play the loop back and ahead, and add layers for lightning, hurricanes, fire, quakes and more.

🚨

Weather alerts Free

National Weather Service alerts as compact cards, with rain and storm notifications in the background.

🧠

AI Briefing tabs NOW freeMore with Premium

A short briefing for each window: NOW, 6–24H and day tabs from 2D to 10D. NOW is free; the longer windows are Premium.

📱

On-device Gemma AI Premium

Optionally download Google's Gemma 3 (1B) model to write briefings on your phone. Your forecast never leaves the device, and it works offline after the download.

🌀

Hurricane tracking Premium

NHC forecast track and cone on the map, plus a HURRICANE briefing tab when a storm is active.

🎥

Weather cams Free

Live cameras near you from USGS, NOAA, Windy.com and WebCOOS coastal cams, plus YouTube live streams.

🛰️

Off-Grid Safety Free

Save your last location and text it to emergency contacts. If your phone supports satellite texting, it can go out with no cell signal. Carrier message and data rates may apply.

Close-One Premium

Pick someone and their place. When a storm hits there, the app drafts a text for you to send.

Tap a tab and get a short, plain-English summary of that window. Built-in briefings are written on your phone from forecast data. With Premium you can download Gemma to write them on-device, or use your own AI provider key.

Radar you can actually read

Classic rain colors, snow that stays light blue, and a timeline you can scrub. Turn on lightning, hurricane, fire or earthquake layers only when you need them.

Weather cams near you, free

Real camera views from USGS, NOAA, Windy.com and WebCOOS coastal cams (SECOORA / NOAA IOOS), plus YouTube live streams. Coastal imagery courtesy of WebCOOS.

Off-Grid Safety, free for everyone

When you're out of signal near a severe alert, the app helps you text your saved location to your emergency contacts. If your phone supports satellite texting, it can go out by satellite. Use Emergency SOS or call 911 first. Satellite texting may require a compatible plan. Carrier message and data rates may apply.
