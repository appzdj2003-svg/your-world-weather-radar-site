Know the storm before it hits. Then tell someone.

Your World Weather Radar is a weather app that warns you early and drafts a text to a person you choose when weather turns dangerous near them. You tap Send. Rain alerts stay free.

  • Early storm alerts and a live rain ETA
  • Close-One: a ready-to-send message for someone you pick
  • Radar, hurricane tracks, tides, and rocket launches on one map
Get it on Google Play

Forecasts are informational. Follow official NWS and local emergency guidance.
