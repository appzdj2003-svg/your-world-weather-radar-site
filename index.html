Radar you can actually read

Classic rain colors, snow that stays light blue, and a timeline you can scrub. Turn on lightning, hurricane, fire or earthquake layers only when you need them.

Off-Grid Safety, free for everyone

When you're out of signal near a severe alert, the app helps you text your saved location to your emergency contacts. If your phone supports satellite texting, it can go out by satellite.
