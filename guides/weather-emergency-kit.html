Build a Basic Weather Emergency Kit

Home / Guides / Emergency kit

Severe storms can knock out power and water for days. Ready.gov recommends a basic kit you can grab quickly.

Basic kit checklist

  • Water: one gallon per person per day for several days.
  • Food: several days' supply of non-perishable food, and a manual can opener.
  • A battery-powered or hand-crank radio, and a NOAA Weather Radio with tone alert.
  • A flashlight, a first-aid kit and extra batteries.
  • A whistle to signal for help, and a dust mask.
  • Moist towelettes, garbage bags and plastic ties for sanitation.
  • A wrench or pliers to turn off utilities, and local maps.
  • A cell phone with chargers and a backup battery.

Don't forget

Prescription medicines, glasses, pet supplies, and copies of important documents. Check your kit and replace expired items regularly.
