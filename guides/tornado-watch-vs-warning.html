Tornado Safety: Watch vs. Warning and Where to Shelter

Home / Guides / Tornado safety

For how watches, warnings and advisories work in general, see Watch vs. warning. Here's what they mean for tornadoes.

  • Tornado Watch: conditions are favorable for tornadoes in and near the watch area. Review your plan and be ready to act.
  • Tornado Warning: a tornado has been sighted or indicated by weather radar. Take shelter immediately.

Where to go

  • A basement, storm cellar, or the lowest floor of a sturdy building.
  • No basement? Pick a small interior room with no windows on the lowest floor, such as a closet, a bathroom or an interior hallway.
  • Get under something sturdy, and cover your head and neck with your arms, a blanket or a helmet.
  • Mobile homes don't offer protection. Plan ahead to get to a sturdy building.

Have more than one way to get warnings

Don't rely on outdoor sirens alone. Use NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts on your phone, local media and apps.
