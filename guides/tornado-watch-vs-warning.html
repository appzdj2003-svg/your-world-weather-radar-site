Tornado watch vs. warning, and where to shelter

Guides / Tornado safety

Watch vs. warning

  • Tornado Watch: conditions are favorable for tornadoes in and near the watch area. Review your plan and be ready to act.
  • Tornado Warning: a tornado has been sighted or indicated by weather radar. Take shelter immediately.

Where to go

  • Go to a basement, storm cellar, or the lowest floor of a sturdy building.
  • No basement? Pick a small interior room with no windows on the lowest floor, such as a closet, bathroom or interior hallway.
  • Get under something sturdy and cover your head and neck with your arms, a blanket or a helmet.
  • Mobile homes do not offer protection. Plan ahead to get to a sturdy building.

Have more than one way to get warnings

Don't rely on outdoor sirens alone. Use NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts on your phone, local media, and apps.

Sources: NWS Tornado Safety, Ready.gov Tornadoes.
