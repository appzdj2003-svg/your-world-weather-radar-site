Flood safety: Turn Around, Don't Drown

Guides / Flood safety

Flooding is one of the deadliest weather hazards in the United States, and many flood deaths happen in vehicles. The NWS campaign is simple: Turn Around, Don't Drown.

Why floodwater is so dangerous

  • Just 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult off their feet.
  • 12 inches of moving water can carry away most cars, and 2 feet can carry away SUVs and trucks.
  • You can't tell how deep water is, or whether the road underneath has washed out.

What to do

  • Never drive around barricades or into flooded roads. Find another route.
  • Be extra careful at night, when it is harder to see flooded roads.
  • During a Flash Flood Warning, move to higher ground right away.
  • Stay out of floodwater on foot. It can hide debris, downed power lines and contamination.

Source: NWS Turn Around Don't Drown.
