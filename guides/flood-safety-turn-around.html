Flood Safety: Turn Around, Don't Drown

Home / Guides / Flood safety

Flooding is one of the deadliest weather hazards in the United States, and many flood deaths happen in vehicles. The National Weather Service's message is simple: Turn Around, Don't Drown.

Why floodwater is so dangerous

  • Just 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult off their feet.
  • 12 inches of moving water can carry away most cars.
  • 2 feet of moving water can carry away SUVs and trucks.

You can't tell how deep the water is, or whether the road underneath has washed out.

What to do

  • Never drive around barricades or into flooded roads. Find another route.
  • Take extra care at night, when flooded roads are harder to see.
  • During a Flash Flood Warning, move to higher ground right away. (For what a watch or a warning means, see Watch vs. warning.)
  • Stay out of floodwater on foot. It can hide debris, downed power lines and contamination.
