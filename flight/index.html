How it works

  • Live position comes from volunteer ADS-B receivers (adsb.lol). Planes outside receiver range won't show.
  • Delay risk is a simple set of rules: FAA ground stops and delay programs, storms, low visibility and strong winds at your airports, hazards on the route, and the plane's own progress. It's an estimate, not airline information.
  • Smooth plane: between live updates (every 20 seconds) we move the plane forward at its last reported speed and heading, for up to 90 seconds. The freshness line says how old the last real position is.
  • Share my flight makes a link that opens this flight with your times filled in, so family can watch it come in.

Live aircraft: adsb.lol (ODbL). Routes: adsb.lol / Virtual Radar Server standing data (CC0); routes are typical for the flight number and can differ on the day. Weather: aviationweather.gov and the National Weather Service. Airport status: FAA NAS Status. Places: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Map imagery: NASA GIBS / Blue Marble.
